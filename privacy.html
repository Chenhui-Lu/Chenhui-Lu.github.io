Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Purpose

This portal provides approved PhD committee members with access to a private research progress dashboard.

Authentication information

Google Sign-In and Cloudflare Access process basic account information—such as name, email address, and profile image—solely to authenticate users and enforce the approved-email access list.

Data use

The portal does not sell personal information or use authentication information for advertising. It does not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, calendars, or other Google account data.

Service providers

Authentication is provided by Google and Cloudflare Access. Their handling of authentication data is governed by their respective privacy policies.
